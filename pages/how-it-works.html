Clean Service Protocol

Our three-phase in-home standards.

Engineered to protect your hardwood, tiles, and living spaces while delivering surgical diagnostic precision.

Phase 01

Home & Floor Protection

  • Neoprene floor runners placed from front entrance to appliance
  • Clean, non-slip protective shoe covers worn at all times
  • Padded tool staging mats prevent counter and tile scratching
  • Protective appliance glides used when pulling machines forward
Phase 02

Instrument-Based Diagnosis

  • Digital multimeter electrical load and continuity testing
  • Infrared thermal imaging to detect overheating motors or coils
  • Oscilloscope verification for complex inverter boards
  • Written explanation of failure cause before parts installation
Phase 03

Spotless Area Handover

  • Complete functional test cycle run with homeowner present
  • Work area wiped down and all packaging removed from premises
  • Defective components safely boxed and removed for recycling
  • Direct digital receipt and warranty certificate issued instantly
Price & Warranty Policy

Fair policies with zero fine print.

Two essential commitments guarantee you never experience unexpected costs or unbacked workmanship.

Diagnostic Credit Toward Approved Repairs

Our flat diagnostic inspection fee covers comprehensive electrical and mechanical troubleshooting. When you approve the itemized quote to perform the repair, that diagnostic fee is applied directly toward your repair total—no double billing.

90-Day Unconditional Parts & Labor Warranty

Every factory OEM replacement component and every minute of technician labor is protected by our written 90-day warranty. If the exact same failure recurs within 90 days, we return promptly to correct the issue with zero additional fees.
