Diagnostics

Common cooling failure symptoms.

When food starts spoiling or frost builds up in the freezer, our specialists isolate the exact component causing the failure.

01

Compartment Not Cooling

Caused by failed evaporator fan motors, clogged condenser coils, or malfunctioning defrost thermostats.

02

Excessive Frost & Ice Buildup

Indicates a failed defrost heater element, stuck bi-metal defrost thermostat, or faulty control board.

03

Water Leakage on Kitchen Floor

Often linked to blocked defrost drain tubes, cracked drain pans, or leaking dual ice maker water valves.

04

Loud Grinding or Clicking Sounds

Typically points to worn fan motor bearings, failing compressor start relays, or damper motor gears.

Inspection Scope

Complete 5-point refrigeration inspection.

Every service visit begins with rigorous testing before any parts are replaced.

01

Airflow & Evaporator Fans

Multimeter testing of fan motor RPM, voltage supply, and damper door operation.

02

Defrost Circuit Elements

Resistance testing of the defrost heater, bi-metal thermostat, and temperature sensors.

03

Compressor & Inverter Board

Capacitor and start relay checks, amperage draw testing, and operating temperature curves.
