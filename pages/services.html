Honest Guidance

Repair or replace? How we help you decide.

We will never push an uneconomical repair on a machine that has reached the end of its useful life.

The 50% Rule of Economic Repair

If the complete repair cost is less than 50% of the replacement price and the unit hasn't passed its expected lifespan, repair provides immediate financial savings.

Lifespan Evaluation Benchmarks

Modern refrigerators last 10–13 years; laundry units 9–12 years; dishwashers 8–10 years. Our technicians evaluate cabinet integrity and motor health before recommending major repairs.

E-Waste Prevention

Repairing an existing machine keeps 150 to 220 pounds of steel, copper, and hazardous refrigerants out of landfills.

Visit Preparation

Simple steps before your technician arrives.

Help us complete your diagnostic inspection quickly and smoothly.

Step 01

Clear the Work Area

Please ensure a clear 3-foot perimeter around your appliance so our specialist can maneuver test instruments and tools safely.

E.g., remove items stored on top of washing machines or in front of ovens.
Step 02

Note Symptoms & Codes

Jot down any error codes displayed on digital screens, unusual humming or grinding sounds, and when the issue first appeared.

This helps our technician match known manufacturer service bulletins.
Step 03

Ensure Utility Access

Ensure the main breaker panel and water shutoff valves (for washers and dishwashers) are accessible for safety isolation during testing.

Our specialist handles all electrical and water disconnects.
