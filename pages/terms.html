Service Agreement

Clear expectations support dependable repairs.

These terms govern appointment requests, on-site diagnostics, estimate authorization, invoicing, and warranty coverage for residential appliance services.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Service Appointments & Arrival Windows

Requested appointment windows are confirmed based on technician routing and availability. Customers agree to provide unobstructed access to the appliance and ensure an authorized adult is present during the diagnostic visit.

2. In-Home Diagnosis & Repair Authorization

A standard diagnostic inspection fee applies to cover technician travel, testing instrumentation, and symptom isolation. Following diagnosis, an itemized quote detailing labor and parts is provided. No repair work commences without explicit customer approval.

3. Replacement Parts & Payment Terms

All installed parts are genuine OEM components or certified manufacturer-approved equivalents. Invoices are due upon completion of authorized repair testing. Itemized digital receipts are archived in your customer portal.

4. Warranty Coverage & Limitations

Completed repairs include a 90-day labor warranty and full manufacturer parts warranty. Warranty coverage applies exclusively to the specific components and systems serviced, excluding pre-existing failures, cosmetic wear, or external utility supply disruptions.

5. Customer Support Contact

For questions regarding invoices, service records, or warranty coverage, contact our support desk at care@fixora.example or call (800) 555-0147.
