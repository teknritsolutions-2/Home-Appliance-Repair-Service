Diagnostic Guide

Why your refrigerator is not cooling.

A practical look at evaporator airflow, frost patterns, defrost timers, start relays, and when professional in-home multi-meter diagnosis is necessary.

1. Inspecting Evaporator Coils & Airflow

When a refrigerator compartment begins warming up while the freezer remains cold, the primary culprit is often restricted airflow. Cold air is produced exclusively in the freezer compartment and circulated upward into the fresh food section via a motorized damper and evaporator fan.

2. The Defrost Circuit Breakdown

If frost blankets the back wall of the freezer compartment, the automatic defrost system has failed. This system consists of three connected components: the defrost timer/board, the bi-metal defrost thermostat, and the heating element. When any single element fails, frost accumulates rapidly, choking off airflow within 48 to 72 hours.

3. Compressor Start Relay & Capacitors

If you hear an intermittent clicking sound coming from the lower rear of the refrigerator every 2–3 minutes accompanied by warm temperatures in both compartments, the compressor start relay or run capacitor has likely failed.
