Data Protection

Your service information deserves careful handling.

This policy explains how Fixora collects, utilizes, and protects information submitted to schedule, diagnose, and document in-home appliance repairs.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable in-home diagnostic and repair service, we collect contact details (name, telephone number, email), residential service addresses, appliance specifications (brand, model, serial number, symptom descriptions), appointment preferences, digital service invoices, and customer communications.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used strictly to dispatch certified technicians, provide live appointment arrival updates, match factory service schematics, generate transparent estimates, maintain warranty records in your customer portal, and process payment transactions.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Retention

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared exclusively with authorized field technicians and essential cloud service providers necessary to fulfill your repair request and maintain digital records for warranty validation.

4. Customer Rights & Inquiries

You may review, update, or request the deletion of your account and service records at any time by contacting our data protection team at privacy@fixora.example.
